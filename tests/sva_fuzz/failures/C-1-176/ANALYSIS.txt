`assert property (@(posedge clk) 1'b1)` -- a property body with no temporal
operators -- is rejected by sva2check with "Skipping top/p0: malformed
spec" (Converted 0 $check cells, 1 unsupported), even though the frontend
already built a correct $check for it.

Consequence: the $check is left driving the frontend's placeholder wire
(frontends/ast/genrtlil.cc deliberately ties an uncompiled temporal
property's A port to an undriven wire that reads x, "loud instead of
passing vacuously"). In sim (-zinit) the x reads as not-1, so the
constant-TRUE assertion fires at every step (see sum.json: fired at all
steps 0..11); under the formal prep, setundef -init -zero turns the same
x into 0 and smtbmc reports a spurious CEX at step 0. Both legs are
equally wrong, so the differential would call this an "agreement" -- the
harness detects the unsupported $check first and buckets it as class C.

Fix direction (not applied): sva2check should recognize a $check whose A
port is not driven by a $sva tree as an already-final combinational check
and leave it alone (count it as converted, not unsupported), or the
frontend should drive a constant property's $check directly.

This shape is emitted by the generator's budget-exhausted fallbacks; it is
fully LRM-legal syntax.
