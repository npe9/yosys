Sequence-level `and` (e.g. `(a ##1 b) and (c ##1 d)`) is LRM-legal
(1800-2009 A.2.10) but sva2check rejects it: "unsupported sequence
operator 'and'". Emitted deliberately at low rate by the generator to
register the gap; both plain and nested occurrences fail the same way.
Note the operator IS reachable today only via this error -- there is no
supported path. Battery t8/t9 avoid it entirely.
